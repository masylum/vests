# v114 card-copy review

Reviewed all 26 active founders and 30 value-card types with the write-card-game-copy skill. This is a wording and portrait update; engine, bot policy, costs and token profiles are unchanged. Bony Ma replaces the name Mony Pa.

Vocabulary and templates:

- **Lead investor** means company control. **Leading company** means furthest along the race track, including ties.
- **Deploy** moves a company’s supply token into the growth bag. **Discarded tokens** are its used tokens; **remove from the game** is permanent removal.
- **Return a share** makes it unissued again. **Take an unissued share** adds it to your holding. Neither operation buys a share.
- **Before the reveal** is the opening of the reveal phase. “During the reveal, once per round” includes that opening and the token reveals. Stefan’s separate limit is once per token reveal.
- Founder “I/me/my” refers to its company. Value-card “you/your” refers to the acting investor. Single-company effects select a portfolio company under the existing general rule; explicit exceptions and multi-company choices remain on the card.
- Retain yellow reveal timing and selective bold effects. Do not introduce new keywords, costs or restrictions.

Clarifications include Reid returning tokens to the bottom of supply, explicit share destinations, investor-only forced sales, and Rights Issue’s affordable mandatory fallback purchase. No additional rules decisions were needed.
