# Dice rules · v157

[Play Vests](http://localhost:5188/) and open **Rules**, **Founders**, or **Value cards** for the live rules and card text.

Each investor starts with an opening share, one opening die, three private value cards, and $20 of repayable capital. The finite deck has eight deal-flow cards per company. Show five cards each round, or as many as remain.

On your turn, pick a public card and hold its auction first. The buyer chooses and rolls two dice immediately after a successful sale. Then the auction caller takes a support action: call capital, hire a founder, play a value card, or pass. If no public cards remain on your turn, go directly to support. The bank rolls two bland dice for each card left after the last turn, without issuing shares.

Roll dice one at a time. A fumbled company discards its next die to recover. Resolve founder reactions before the next die. Companies cannot move below space 1. At round end, roll the bank dice, resolve scheduled founder powers, then move all companies tied for last one space. Once-per-round founder limits apply throughout the round. The game ends after the round in which a company reaches 24, or after the final deal-flow row and its round.

The exit pays $1 per share at space 5 and rises to $20 per share at 24. Secret investment bonuses apply at exit. Called capital is deducted from the final score.
